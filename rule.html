Basic Rules of Soccer

Soccer is played by two teams. Each team has 11 players. The goal is to score more goals than the other team.

1. Number of Players

Each team has 11 players on the field, including one goalkeeper.

2. Match Time

A normal soccer match lasts 90 minutes. It has two halves, and each half lasts 45 minutes.

3. Hands and Arms

Players cannot normally touch the ball with their hands or arms. The goalkeeper can use their hands inside their own penalty area.

4. Yellow Card

A yellow card is a warning from the referee. It is given when a player breaks certain rules.

5. Red Card

A red card means a player must leave the game. Their team cannot replace that player.

6. Penalty Kick

A penalty kick is given for certain fouls inside the penalty area.

How to Win?

The team that scores more goals wins the match. If both teams score the same number of goals, the match is a draw.
