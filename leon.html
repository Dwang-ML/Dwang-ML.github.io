Football?

Football is a sport played by two teams of eleven players on a grass field. The aim is to kick the ball into the other team's goal. Players cannot touch the ball with their hands, only the goalkeeper can. A match lasts ninety minutes, and the team with more goals wins. It is the most popular sport in the world, with billions of fans.
