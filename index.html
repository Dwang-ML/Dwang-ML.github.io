What is Football?

Soccer is one of the most popular sports in the world. It is played by two teams, and each team has 11 players. The main goal is to score by kicking the ball into the other team's net. Players use their feet, head, and body to control the ball. Soccer requires teamwork, speed, and skill. It is not only a sport but also a way for people to make friends and enjoy playing together.
