Soccer World

Learn about the most famous competitions in the world.

FIFA World Cup

The FIFA World Cup is the biggest football competition. National teams from different countries compete for the trophy. It is held every four years.

UEFA European Championship

The UEFA European Championship is also called the EURO. European national teams compete for the championship. It is usually held every four years.

Premier League

The Premier League is a famous football league in England. Many famous clubs play in this competition.
